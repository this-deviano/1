## M2 task breakdown (TASK-036 — docs only; starts only after M1 exit is ratified)

Design law for M2: a Unit is a **model-level pure function** with a **WebAudio projection**.
The model owns parameters and state; the projection is derived from the model and is the
only place WebAudio nodes are constructed. E-006 applies per Unit: one definition of the
Unit's maths, one projection, no field-subset mirrors.

### FINDINGS — 4 root causes behind 8 failing criteria (none threshold-shopped)

- **FND-01 — the export exceeds full scale (AUDIO-side; the only audio finding).** True peak +1.65 dBFS, 250 samples/channel strictly over 1.0, both channels, stable across four runs. No ledger row recorded export headroom before: parity and determinism track the Δ *between* paths, not absolute level, and the metered readout tracks the live bus. Consequence: this mix clips on any integer DAC and would clip in a 16/24-bit render. Hypothesis: comp (−6 dB / 4:1 / 3 ms attack) + summed drum+keys transients + MASTER_GAIN 0.9 leaves no headroom, and a 3 ms attack passes the transient. The remedy is headroom/limiting or a gain decision — **parameter work, M2 scope, deliberately not attempted here.**
- **FND-02 — a whole-file `|DC| ≤ 1e-4` is the wrong instrument for these files (INSTRUMENT-side).** Export segment means `[7.08e-4, 3.70e-4, −2.17e-4, −2.26e-4, 3.00e-4, −1.03e-4, 1.32e-4, 0]` — alternating sign, 4 of 8 sharing the overall sign, concentration 5.88, final segment exactly 0 (the digital-silence tail). A constant bias cannot produce that pattern; the figure is the partial-cycle integral of low-frequency decaying voices over the window (≈1e-3 for a 55 Hz voice at 0.5 over ~0.5 s). The take's 2.3e-2 is likewise the mean of a burst train inside silence. **Recommendation:** judge DC per segment (or over an integer number of cycles, or after DC blocking) — never on a whole-file mean of a non-stationary file.
- **FND-03 — the interior-p99.9 lattice criterion over-rejects on transient-rich material and is vacuous on sparse material (INSTRUMENT-side).** Export: 5 flagged of 3,309 boundary samples, but 0.1 % of 3,309 = **3.31 are expected by chance**; Poisson P(X ≥ 5 | λ = 3.31) ≈ 0.24 → **no evidence of an engine seam.** The excess is also small (0.06885 vs 0.05941, +16 % — the file's loudest transient landing on a boundary sample), not the order-of-magnitude jump a seam would be. Take: interior p99.9 = 0 (440–777 nonzero samples in digital silence), so the criterion passes trivially and says nothing. **Recommendation:** compare the outlier count against the chance baseline this run now records (`expectedOutliersByChance`), or use a local-neighbourhood statistic; flag the check uninformative when the interior p99.9 is 0.
- **FND-04 — the fake device cannot exercise the trim or level criteria (INSTRUMENT-side).** Three runs, three takes: `1a55996e…` (33,185 frames, 777 FS samples, onset 0.0 ms), `1fd30a76…` (29,089 frames, 440, onset 446.4 ms), `3603ca04…` (33,185 frames, 557, onset 0.0 ms). Chromium's fake device emits a short full-scale 440 Hz square burst inside digital silence, so after `cmdFinishTake` trims 2.034 s (4 beats @ 118 qpm) from an arbitrary capture start, the residual window's leading silence reflects the **beep's phase, not the trim** — and the peak is exactly 1.0 by construction, so `clipped == 0` is unreachable. The ±50 ms criterion is therefore neither satisfiable nor failable on this instrument. **Recommendation:** drive trim/level forensics from a continuous, non-full-scale source (a WAV fed as the fake capture device) and keep real-hardware behaviour in HV-DEFERRED-02 where it belongs. The **export's** trim check is the meaningful one and it passes.
